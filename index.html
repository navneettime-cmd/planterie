Make Your Own Tiny World

Spend a calm Sunday morning at our plant store in Panchsheel Park, building a living terrarium with your own hands. From the first stone to the last leaf, all yours.

₹300 booking fee. Terrarium materials are paid separately at the studio.

How the Morning Goes

Settle In01

Find a seat among the plants, order a coffee if you like, and pick the few plants you want living in your jar. Your guide is right there if you'd like a second opinion.

Build the Ground02

Lay down stones, then soil, then a soft bed of moss. Layer by layer, you build the ground your little world will live on.

Make It Yours03

Place each plant where you want it, shape the landscape, and step back to look. You take home something living that you made by hand.

What's Included

  • An 8-inch fishbowl glass jar
  • Live plants, soil layers, moss and stones
  • A guide beside you for the full hour
  • Care instructions to take home

Who It's For

For anyone who has kept a plant alive on a windowsill, slowed down to look at someone else's garden, or wished their weekends had a little more green in them. You don't need to call yourself a plant person. You just need to like the idea of growing something of your own.

No experience needed, and it's for adults. Come on your own, bring a friend, or make it a Sunday date that isn't another brunch. One hour with your hands busy and your phone down.
